FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EquaCore Digital do?+

EquaCore Digital helps organisations improve service delivery and digital operations. We provide platform implementation and improvement across Halo and Freshworks solutions, independent ServiceNow advisory and day-2 managed operations, specialist talent, and responsible AI delivery. EquaCore is a Full Halo Technology Alliance Partner, Freshworks Partner, Anthropic Partner and OpenAI Partner.

What ServiceNow and Halo roles can EquaCore place?+

We place administrators and system engineers, developers and integration engineers, solution architects, business analysts, CMDB/Discovery/SAM specialists, and process consultants across ServiceNow and HaloITSM, HaloPSA, and HaloCRM, including certified Halo administrators, HaloITSM implementation specialists, and Halo Service Desk engineers. All candidates are technically assessed and enterprise-ready before placement.

Is EquaCore an official Halo partner?+

Yes. EquaCore Digital is a full Halo Technology Alliance Partner, authorised to implement and support all three Halo products: HaloITSM (IT and Enterprise Service Management), HaloPSA (Professional Services Automation for MSPs), and HaloCRM (omnichannel CRM for customer-facing teams). We deliver platform implementations, legacy migrations, process alignment, user onboarding, and post-go-live managed support across the complete Halo product suite.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?+

Yes. While our talent base is primarily Nigerian, our delivery is global. We work with clients in the UK, Europe, and across West Africa, providing distributed teams with structured governance and measurable outcomes.

What is the difference between HaloITSM, HaloPSA, and HaloCRM?+

All three are products from Halo Service Solutions built on the same platform, but designed for different audiences. HaloITSM is ITIL-aligned IT and Enterprise Service Management — ideal for organisations managing internal IT services, facilities, HR, and more. HaloPSA (Professional Services Automation) is built for managed service providers and technology companies that need to manage client tickets, billing, contracts, time tracking, and projects in one place. HaloCRM is an omnichannel customer relationship platform for sales, marketing, and customer service teams. As a full Halo partner, EquaCore can implement and support any or all three.

What engagement models does EquaCore offer?+

We offer project-based delivery (defined scope and milestones), ongoing managed services (monthly retainer), embedded consulting and staff augmentation (professionals integrated into your team), and hybrid models that combine delivery with knowledge transfer.

HaloITSM

IT & Enterprise Service Management

A unified, ITIL-aligned platform — every module included as standard with no per-feature licensing. Purpose-built for organisations running IT, HR, facilities, and finance service desks on one platform.

Incident & Problem Management

ITIL-aligned incident handling with SLA management, AI-powered triage, and root cause investigation — reducing repeat failures and cutting resolution times.

Change & Release Management

CAB workflows, standardised change procedures, and disciplined release tracking — from minor fixes to major platform rollouts.

CMDB & Asset Management

Track configuration items, visualise CI dependencies, and auto-discover assets — giving your team a reliable foundation for all service management decisions.

Service Catalogue & Self-Service Portal

ITIL-compliant service catalogue with a fully brandable self-service portal — users find and request services without calling the help desk.

Knowledge Management

Rich knowledge base with AI-generated articles, full-text search, and articles surfaced to users before they raise a ticket — deflecting volume at the source.

Automation & Halo AI

No-code workflow automation, AI triage, Emotion AI, Virtual Agent, and automated resolutions for routine tickets — reducing manual workload at scale.

Major Incident & Project Management

A dedicated Major Incident console with 5C coordination (Command, Control, Comms, Coordination, Cooperation) — and full project portfolio tooling built in.

Enterprise Service Management: HaloITSM extends beyond IT — HR, Facilities, Finance, and Legal teams can all operate service desks on the same platform, sharing a single system of record.

All modules included: Unlike many competitors, Halo does not charge per module. Every capability listed here — including Halo AI — is included as standard in the licence.

HaloPSA

Professional Services Automation for MSPs

The all-inclusive PSA platform built for managed service providers, cloud solution providers, and telecoms companies. Consolidate ticketing, billing, contracts, projects, and CRM — eliminating the need for multiple disconnected tools.

Service Desk & Multi-Client Ticketing

MSP-focused ticketing with SLA management, automated workflows, and centralised multi-client communications — all in one console.

Billing, Contracts & Invoicing

Zero-touch invoice creation, contract renewal automation, and per-agreement billing rules — integrated with leading accounting platforms including Xero and QuickBooks.

Time Tracking

Automatically capture time across all customer interaction points — ensuring accurate pay-as-you-go billing and genuine project cost visibility.

Sales & CRM

Pipeline management, opportunity tracking, and full contact history — helping MSPs grow accounts and retain customers with data-driven insight.

Project Management

From simple tasks to complex client portfolios — real-time project dashboards, resource planning, and automated Statement of Work generation.

Asset & Stock Management

Track client assets and configuration items, manage stock levels and costing, and log incidents directly against assets for rapid diagnosis.

Who is HaloPSA for? Managed Service Providers, Telecoms companies, Cloud Solution Providers, consultancy firms, and software businesses that need a single operational platform to manage clients, delivery, and finance.

Competing with: ConnectWise, Autotask, Atera, and Kaseya BMS — at lower total cost of ownership, with every module included as standard from day one.

Privacy Policy

How EquaCore Digital collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 4 July 2026  ·  Data Controller: EquaCore Digital Ltd (registered in Nigeria)  ·  Contact: Info@equacoredigital.com

1. Who we are

EquaCore Digital Ltd ("EquaCore", "we", "us", "our") is a digital operations consulting firm incorporated in the Federal Republic of Nigeria, with global operations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect personal information when you use equacoredigital.com, submit our enquiry or talent pool forms, or otherwise interact with us.

Because our operations are global and we engage with candidates, clients, and partners across multiple jurisdictions, we hold ourselves to the higher of the applicable standards. Specifically, we comply with:

  • The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), enforced by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) — our primary regulatory framework
  • The UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 — for data subjects in the United Kingdom
  • The EU General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR) — for data subjects in the European Economic Area
  • Other applicable data-protection laws of the jurisdiction in which a data subject is resident, where stricter

2. Information we collect

2.1 When you submit our contact form

  • Name, email address, organisation
  • Platform of interest (optional)
  • The content of your message

2.2 When you join our Talent Pool

The Talent Pool registration form on our website collects the following and submits it to our workflow-automation platform, n8n Cloud, which processes it on our behalf:

  • Full name and email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Country / location
  • LinkedIn profile URL (optional)
  • Primary platform / specialism, years of experience, availability, and engagement preference
  • Certifications (required) and a cover note (optional)
  • Your CV (PDF or Word)
  • Your consent to us storing these details for recruitment purposes, recorded with your submission

2.3 When you book a demo

Before we show you our booking calendar, we ask for a few details so we know who we’re meeting. Through the booking form we collect:

  • Your name and work email
  • Company / organisation (optional)

These details are processed by n8n Cloud and stored in our SharePoint records. When you then choose a time, the booking itself is handled by Microsoft Bookings, which collects the meeting details you provide in order to schedule the call.

2.4 When you visit our website

  • Standard analytics data via Google Analytics 4: anonymised IP, page views, device type, referral source, session duration
  • No advertising cookies, no cross-site tracking

5. Who we share your data with

We share your information only with the parties below, and only to the extent necessary:

  • Cloudflare — hosts our website and proxies all visitor traffic through its global edge network. Cloudflare processes IP addresses, request headers, and basic technical metadata for the purposes of content delivery, caching, and security (DDoS / bot protection). GDPR-compliant via Cloudflare’s Data Processing Addendum and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
  • n8n Cloud (n8n GmbH) — our workflow-automation platform. It processes contact-form, demo-booking, and talent-pool submissions made on our website on our behalf, routing your data (including your CV) between our forms, our SharePoint records, and our outbound email provider. Based in the European Union (Germany), GDPR-compliant.
  • Microsoft — we use Microsoft 365 email and SharePoint document storage to receive and store enquiries, talent pool registrations, and CVs in our private organisational tenant, and Microsoft Bookings to schedule the demo meetings you request.
  • Zoho Corporation — sends our transactional confirmation emails — for example, the acknowledgement you receive after submitting the contact form or joining the talent pool. Zoho processes your name and email address solely for the purpose of delivering that message. GDPR-compliant.
  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymised website analytics only. GDPR-compliant via Google’s Data Processing Addendum.
  • Potential employers (talent pool only) — we share your candidate profile with a specific employer only after obtaining your explicit consent for that specific opportunity. We never share talent pool data to a general database.

We do not sell your data, ever. We do not use your data for advertising or profile-based targeting.

6. International transfers

EquaCore is incorporated in Nigeria and our operations are global. Some of our service providers (Cloudflare, n8n, Microsoft, Zoho, Google) are based outside Nigeria, the United Kingdom, and the European Economic Area — principally in the United States and the European Union. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on:

  • For all data subjects: contractual safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses) with each processor, in line with Sections 41–43 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023
  • For UK data subjects: the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or addendum to EU SCCs, as approved by the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO)
  • For EU/EEA data subjects: European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Adequacy decisions issued by the NDPC, the UK ICO, or the European Commission, where applicable

We review these arrangements regularly to ensure your data continues to receive equivalent protection regardless of where it is processed.

7. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and where applicable the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("the right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we process your data
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where consent is the lawful basis (e.g. talent pool)
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Lodge a complaint — with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng); UK data subjects may complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk); EU/EEA data subjects may complain to their national supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, email Info@equacoredigital.com. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge unless your request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

8. Cookies

We use only essential cookies and Google Analytics. Specifically:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function (none are set unless strictly necessary).
  • Analytics cookies — Google Analytics 4 (_ga, _ga_*) for anonymised usage statistics. IP anonymisation is enabled.

We do not use advertising cookies, social-media tracking, or cross-site profiling. You can disable cookies in your browser without affecting site functionality.

9. Security

We protect your data using:

  • TLS / HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
  • Microsoft 365 enterprise security for stored data (encryption at rest, access logging)
  • Role-based access controls limiting which EquaCore personnel can view your data
  • Regular review of our security and data-handling practices

No system is perfectly secure. If you become aware of a security incident affecting your data, please email Info@equacoredigital.com immediately. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (and, where applicable, the UK ICO or relevant EU supervisory authority) and affected individuals within 72 hours, as required by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the UK/EU GDPR.

10. Children’s data

This website and our services are not directed to individuals under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our practices evolve or as required by law. The "last updated" date at the top of the page reflects the most recent change. Material changes affecting how we use existing data will be communicated by email where we have your contact details.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions, data subject requests, or complaints:

  • Email: Info@equacoredigital.com
  • Subject line for data requests: Privacy Request — [Your Name]

If you are not satisfied with our response, you have the right to complain to your supervisory authority:

  • Nigeria (primary): Nigeria Data Protection Commission — ndpc.gov.ng
  • United Kingdom: Information Commissioner’s Office — ico.org.uk · phone 0303 123 1113
  • European Union / EEA: your national data protection authority (find yours at edpb.europa.eu)
